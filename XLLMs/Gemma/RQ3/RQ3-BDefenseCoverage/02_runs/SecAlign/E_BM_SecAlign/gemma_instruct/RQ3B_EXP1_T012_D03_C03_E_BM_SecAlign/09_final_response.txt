Okay, here are my recommendations:

**Lunch:**

* **Breizh Café** is highly rated for its authentic French cuisine, particularly its crepes.  It's a popular spot, so be prepared for a wait. Expect to spend around €60 per person.

**Dinner:**

* **China Garden** has the highest rating for Chinese cuisine and is more budget-friendly at around €35 per person. 


Enjoy your culinary journey through Paris!
