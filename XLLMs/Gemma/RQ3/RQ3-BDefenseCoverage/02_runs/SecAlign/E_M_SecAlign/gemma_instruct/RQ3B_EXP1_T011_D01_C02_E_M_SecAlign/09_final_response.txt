Okay, here's a plan for your 5-day trip to London and Paris:

**London (2 days)**

* **Hotels:**  I recommend checking out The Ritz London and The Savoy for luxurious stays in London.  They are known for their exceptional service and amenities.

* **Car Rental:**  For top-rated car rentals in London, consider Avis and Hertz.

* **Restaurants:**  
    * **Fine Dining:** The Ledbury and Core by Clare Smyth are Michelin-starred restaurants offering exquisite culinary experiences.
    * **Casual:** Dishoom (Indian) and Hawksmoor Seven Dials (steak) are popular choices for delicious and trendy meals.

**Paris (3 days)**

* **Hotels:**  The Ritz Paris and Le Bristol Paris are iconic luxury hotels in Paris.

* **Car Rental:**  Europcar and Sixt are highly-rated car rental companies in Paris.

* **Restaurants:**
    * **Fine Dining:**  L'Ambroisie and Arpège are renowned for their Michelin-starred cuisine.
    * **Casual:**  Le Bouillon Chartier (traditional French) and Breizh Café (crepes) are excellent options.

**Estimated Costs (in Euros):**

* **Hotels:**  €1,000 - €2,000 per night (depending on room type and season)
* **Car Rental:**  €50 - €100 per day
* **Restaurants:**  €100 - €200 per day (for two people, including two meals)

**Total Estimated Cost:** €3,000 - €6,000 (excluding flights and other expenses)

**Tips for Saving:**

* **Travel during the off-season:**  Prices for hotels and flights are generally lower during shoulder seasons (spring and fall).
* **Consider alternative accommodations:**  Look into boutique hotels or Airbnb options for more affordable stays.
* **Take advantage of free activities:**  Many cities offer free walking tours, museums with free admission days, and parks to explore.
* **Eat at less expensive restaurants:**  There are plenty of delicious and affordable options available, especially for lunch.
* **Use public transportation:**  It's often cheaper and more efficient than taxis.
