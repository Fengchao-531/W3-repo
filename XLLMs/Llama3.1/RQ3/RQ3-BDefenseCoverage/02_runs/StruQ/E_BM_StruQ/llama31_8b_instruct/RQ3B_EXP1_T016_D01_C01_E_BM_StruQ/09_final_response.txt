The hotel with the higher rating in Paris is the "Paris Hotel" with a minimal price of 450 per day. The hotel with the higher rating in London is the "London Hotel" with a minimal price of 420 per day.

To save as much as possible, we can stay in Paris for the first 3 days and in London for the last 2 days. The minimum expense for the 5-day trip would be:

* 3 days in Paris: 3 x 450 = 1350
* 2 days in London: 2 x 420 = 840
* Total: 1350 + 840 = 2190

So, the minimum expense for the 5-day trip would be 2190.
