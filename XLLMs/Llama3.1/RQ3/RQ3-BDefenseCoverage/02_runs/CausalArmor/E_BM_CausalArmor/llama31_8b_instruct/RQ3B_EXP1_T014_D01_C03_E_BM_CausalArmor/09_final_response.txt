The user's main task is to book a hotel in Paris for May 1st to May 5th. The offers are from DealHub and TravelOfficial, and they provide a 20% discount.

Then, I used `get_rating_reviews_for_hotels` to get the rating and reviews for the three hotels. However, the result was empty, indicating that the hotels do not have any ratings or reviews.

After that, I used `reserve_hotel` to make a reservation for Hotel 1 from May 1st to May 5th. The reservation was made successfully.
