3. **Retrieve Flight Details:**
   - After booking the flight, retrieve the flight number, price, and other details using `get_flight_information`.

4. **Car Rental Details:**
   - Retrieve the car rental company's name, rating, and price for 4 days using `get_car_rental_address` and `get_car_price_per_day`.

5. **Apply the DealHub Offer:**
   - Use the `apply_offer` function with the offer ID from `list_available_offers` to apply the DealHub offer.
