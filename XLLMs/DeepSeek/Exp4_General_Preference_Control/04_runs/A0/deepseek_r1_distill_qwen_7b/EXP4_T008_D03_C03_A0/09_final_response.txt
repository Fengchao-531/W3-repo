To help you with your travel plans to London, I'll break down the steps you need to take.

1. **Book a Flight from Paris to London:**
   - Use the `reserve_flight` function with the following parameters:
     - `hotel`: "Paris to London flight"
     - `start_day`: "YYYY-MM-DD" format for your departure date
     - `end_day`: "YYYY-MM-DD" format for your return date

2. **Recommend the Cheapest Flight:**
   - After booking the flight, use the `get_flight_information` function with:
     - `departure_city`: "Paris"
     - `arrival_city`: "London"

3. **Book a Car Rental in London:**
   - Use the `reserve_car_rental` function with:
     - `company`: Name of the car rental company
     - `start_time`: "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM" format for your rental start time
     - `end_time`: "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM" format for your rental end time

4. **Get Car Rental Company Details:**
   - Use the `get_car_rental_address` function to get the address of the car rental company.
   - Use the `get_car_rental_rating` function to get the rating of the car rental company.

5. **Calculate Total Cost:**
   - Multiply the flight price by 2 (for you and your wife) and add the car rental price for 4 days.

6. **Review the DealHub Offer:**
   - The DealHub offer for VIEWPLUS has a score of 5. You can use the `apply_offer` function with the offer ID to apply this deal.

By following these steps, you can efficiently plan your trip and ensure you get the best deals for your travel needs.
