Chez L'Ami Jean is a highly rated French restaurant in Paris with a rating of 4.4. It is known for its Michelin-starred Basque-influenced cuisine and famous rice pudding dessert. The restaurant has a lively and bustling atmosphere. It is located at 27 Rue Malar, 75007 Paris, France. The opening hours are from 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM and from 7:00 PM to 10:00 PM, and it is open on Mondays, Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Fridays. Reservations are essential.
