For lunch, based on the ratings and reviews, **Breizh Café** is highly recommended. It has a rating of 3.9 and is known for its best crepes in Paris, both sweet and savory. The restaurant uses authentic Breton cider and artisanal ingredients. However, it can get quite busy, especially during peak hours, so you might want to consider arriving early. They also offer gluten-free buckwheat crepes. The ambiance is great, and the staff is friendly. The cost for lunch is approximately €60 per person.

For dinner, **Chez L'Ami Jean** is a top choice with a rating of 4.4. It is famous for its Michelin-starred Basque-influenced cuisine and a famous rice pudding dessert. The restaurant has a lively and bustling atmosphere, but reservations are essential. The cost for dinner is approximately €24 per person.

Both restaurants provide a good dining experience, but Chez L'Ami Jean might offer a slightly better view due to its Michelin star status and unique cuisine. However, the choice ultimately depends on your personal preference.
