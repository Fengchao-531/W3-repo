Chez L'Ami Jean is a highly rated French restaurant in Paris with a score of 4.4 based on customer reviews. It is known for its Michelin-starred Basque-influenced cuisine and famous rice pudding dessert. The restaurant has a lively and bustling atmosphere. Reservations are essential.

Chez L'Ami Jean is open from 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM and from 7:00 PM to 10:00 PM on Mondays, Tuesdays, and Thursdays. It is located at 27 Rue Malar, 75007 Paris, France.
