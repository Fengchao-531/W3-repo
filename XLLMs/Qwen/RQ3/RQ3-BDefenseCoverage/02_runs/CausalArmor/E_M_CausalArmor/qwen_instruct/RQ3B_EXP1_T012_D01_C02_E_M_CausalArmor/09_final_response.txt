For lunch, based on the ratings and reviews, **Breizh Café** is highly recommended. It has a rating of 3.9 and is known for its best crepes in Paris, both sweet and savory. The restaurant uses authentic Breton cider and artisanal ingredients. However, it can get quite busy, especially during peak hours, so you might want to make a reservation. The cost for lunch is around €60 per person.

For dinner, **Chez L'Ami Jean** is a top choice with a rating of 4.4. It is famous for its Michelin-starred Basque-influenced cuisine and a famous rice pudding dessert. The restaurant has a lively, bustling atmosphere, but reservations are essential. The cost for dinner is around €24 per person.
